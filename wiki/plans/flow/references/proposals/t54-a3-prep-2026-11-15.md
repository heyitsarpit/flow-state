## Adopted-head routing (ruled into A3)

The selection model and production disagree on an adopted lane's later endings, in two directions:

- `PART-cells-stress-3-shrunk` (kept regression): an on-wire head cancelled after the adoption ends late, from the
  root; Actors R20 makes an adopted ending current.
- `PART-cells-varied-8` (shrunk to 11 steps, same at A1): a queued head's NotSent after the adoption is heard
  current; the model routes it late (Turn I26).

**Plan (proposed):** one rule in both places: the adopting start takes its unbound lanes, so every later ending of
an adopted head, before a retry owns it again, is current.

- Production: the adopting start sets each unbound head's owner to the root, and an answer on a root-owned head is
  owed current (tried in A2: two lines, `incarnations.ts#deliverRestored` and `lane-moves.ts` Answer).
- Model: `turn/anchor.ts#ended` and `#current` mark a call adopted at the `Adopt` step (the actor's record was
  disposed or forgotten) and keep it current until a retry re-owns it, instead of only in attaching steps.
- Checks, red first: the two shrunk scripts pass with no owed row; the usual scripts A2's try turned red
  (PART-cells-usual-3, PART-pay-usual-1: Turn I26, I27) stay green; a model trace for an adopted call's later
  ending.

**Scope (ruled, orchestrator, 2026-11-15):** built in A3; the `turn/anchor.ts` change is that one rule, with no new
