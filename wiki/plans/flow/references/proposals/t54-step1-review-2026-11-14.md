## Blocking findings (requirement text, owner: T54 building lead)

- **F1. A-2 checks only one of the three suspend moments.** Actors I41 and Actors T22 name three: an incarnation's
  end, `disposeActor`, and Runtime disposal. A-2's check and its hardening cover only Runtime disposal. On
  today's code, an ending with no lane that is still in the mail when its incarnation is gone is dropped silently
  at `src/core/serve.ts:267-272` (`onNone: () => undefined`). Both kinds of row are lost that way: a decline's
  ending owed to the identity, which should go Waiting, and a read's ending owed to that incarnation alone, which
  should close Recorded (Actors I41's second clause; consult 3, `suspend`). The mail gets there through X-4's
  backlog, or when a turn both declines and ends its actor. This is a lost ending in T54's range.
  **Fix:** extend A-2 over all three moments. Assert that the decline's ending is heard exactly once, and that an
  ending owed to one incarnation gives exactly one Recorded. The wrong runtime is `ledger.suspend-skips-laneless`,
  applied at an incarnation's end.
- **F2. A decline from the lane table carries a lane today.** `Decline` at `src/core/lane-moves.ts:207-218` gives
  the ending `lane: Option.some(at.id)`. Only the declines before the table (Unencodable, NoPersistence;
  `lanes.ts:94-105`) have no lane. WriteLanes R10 and the `model`'s Owed say a declined commit has neither an
  Attempt nor a lane. If a Rejected decline's turn is refused twice, `keepEnding` (`lanes.ts:258-272`) keeps it on
  the busy lane. Under `keptOn` that row would block the key, so later commits would be declined Rejected with no
  cause: a made-up ending. A-7's hardening ("a laneless Kept row blocks no key") assumes laneless declines but
  never tests a table decline.
  **Fix:** in A-7 (or A-9), make the decline a Rejected decline from the table. Refuse its turn twice, let the
  head resolve, then commit on the key: it mints. Also add the opposite case: a never-sent Attempt's NotSent row
  keeps its lane and, when Kept, blocks. While Open, today it is born with no lane (`lane-moves.ts:103-107`).

## Item 4: the two Spec fixes planned for A3

- **WriteAhead I24 against WriteAhead R5 and T18:** a contradiction of wording. Use the Spec-contradiction rule;
  it is not a guarantee change. I24 says `inFlight` is true when the head "was acknowledged". An Acknowledging head
  has not been acknowledged yet. R5 ("Acknowledging to Cancelling: it may be on the wire") and T18 (saved
  Acknowledging means true) say true. R5's `must` already fixes the range, so editing I24 to match it changes no

  "never sent" does not conflict, since never sent and possibly on the wire after the landing are both true.
  The building lead decides and adds a spec-log line.

  ambiguous. **Proposed:** the "as built for reject" list is the exact Schema today. The other fields keep their
  owners: subject path T17, releasing mark T18, identity version T9 and T20. Deleting any of them from the target
  list is a guarantee change and goes to the user. T54 adds no field (Must not decide alone).
