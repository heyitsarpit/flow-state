# Reimagine Range

Use this skill to find methods from other fields that might solve your problem, and propose how to
test them here. A problem in another field may resemble yours under a different name; this skill
looks for those techniques, picks the few that would really transfer, and proposes how to
test them cheaply.

## Steps

1. **Describe what the work must make possible, not the proposed solution.** Say what the work must
   make possible, keeping its goal and constraints, in terms another field would recognize.

2. **Look in other fields.** Find fields that solve similar problems and research their concrete
   methods and tools. Borrow the method itself; adopting an expert's role or jargon does not
   transfer anything.

3. **Pick at most three useful techniques.** For each, explain how it connects to your problem, what
   inputs it needs, what has to be true for it to transfer, and what applying it would cost.

4. **Propose how to test each one:** an independent check or a small, bounded experiment, with the
   observable result that would show whether it is worth adopting.

## What to return

The techniques, their sources, the limits on where they apply, and the experiment you recommend
running next. Do not widen the agreed scope or run the experiments without authorization.
